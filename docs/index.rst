How to Get Big Fish Games Billing Support
=========================================

If you have a billing problem with Big Fish Games, using the correct
support process can help you resolve the issue more efficiently.
Billing problems may include unexpected charges, missing purchases,
payment failures, refund requests, or Game Club charges.

This guide explains how to contact Big Fish Games billing support,
what information to prepare, and how to describe a missing or
incorrect purchase.

Start With Big Fish Games Support
---------------------------------

The first step is to visit the official Big Fish Games Help Center.
Search the support articles for your specific billing problem before
submitting a support request.

Common billing questions include:

* Missing game purchases
* Unexpected charges
* Refund requests
* Payment-method problems
* Game Club billing
* PayPal payment issues
* Problems accessing a purchased game

If the available support articles do not solve your problem, use the
official customer-support request option.

Describe Your Billing Problem Clearly
-------------------------------------

When contacting Big Fish Games support, explain exactly what happened.
Include the date of the transaction, the amount charged, and the name
of the game whenever possible.

For example::

   I was charged $9.99 on August 10 for a game purchase, but the
   purchase does not appear in my Big Fish Games account.

This statement clearly explains the problem and gives the support team
important information about the transaction.

Check Your Purchase History
---------------------------

If a game does not appear in your Big Fish Games account, first check
your Purchase History.

Make sure you are signed in with the same email address that was used
when the game was purchased. If you have more than one Big Fish Games
account, check the other accounts as well.

You should also search your email inbox for the purchase confirmation.
The confirmation email can help you identify the account and order
associated with the transaction.

If you cannot find the purchase after checking your accounts and email,
contact Big Fish Games customer support.

Information to Provide to Support
----------------------------------

Provide enough information for the support team to identify the
transaction.

Useful information includes:

* Big Fish Games account email address
* Purchase date
* Amount charged
* Game name
* Order or confirmation number
* Payment method used
* Billing address, if requested
* Other email addresses that may have been used

For payment-card problems, provide only the limited card information
requested by the official support process. Never provide your complete
card number, CVV, password, or security code.

Example Billing Support Request
-------------------------------

You can use the following format when contacting Big Fish Games
support::

   Subject: Big Fish Games Purchase Missing From Account

   Hello Big Fish Games Support,

   I need assistance with a purchase that does not appear in my
   account.

   Account email: [email address]
   Purchase date: August 10
   Amount charged: $9.99
   Game name: [game name]
   Order number: [order number]

   I was charged $9.99 on August 10 for a game purchase, but the
   purchase does not appear in my Big Fish Games account.

   I have checked my purchase history and still cannot locate the
   transaction.

   Please review the transaction and help me locate the purchase.

   Thank you for your assistance.

Payment Method Problems
-----------------------

If the problem involves a payment method rather than a missing
purchase, check whether your card or PayPal information is current.

An expired card, declined payment, or outdated payment method may
prevent a purchase from being completed.

If you recently changed your payment information, make sure the
updated information is associated with the correct type of purchase.
Recurring services and individual purchases may use different billing
settings.

Game Club Billing Issues
------------------------

If you notice a recurring charge, check whether it is associated with
a Big Fish Games Game Club membership.

Review your account information and previous emails to determine
whether the charge relates to a membership.

If you believe the charge is incorrect, include the following
information in your support request::

   Account email: [email address]
   Charge date: [date]
   Amount charged: [$ amount]
   Service: Game Club
   Order or confirmation number: [number]

   I do not recognize this charge and would like assistance
   determining what it is associated with.

Refund Requests
---------------

If you want a refund, review the current Big Fish Games refund policy
before contacting support. Refund eligibility may depend on the
purchase date, product, payment method, and other circumstances.

A refund request can be written as follows::

   Subject: Big Fish Games Refund Request

   Hello Big Fish Games Support,

   I would like assistance with a refund request.

   Account email: [email address]
   Game purchased: [game name]
   Purchase date: [date]
   Amount paid: [$ amount]
   Order number: [order number]

   Reason for refund:
   [Briefly explain the reason.]

   Please review this purchase and let me know whether it is eligible
   for a refund and what steps I need to follow.

   Thank you for your assistance.

Mobile Purchase Billing
-----------------------

If you purchased a Big Fish Games product through a mobile application,
determine which platform processed the payment.

For example, purchases made through an iPhone or iPad application may
be processed through Apple, while Android purchases may be processed
through Google Play.

Check your purchase receipt to determine the payment provider. If
Apple or Google processed the payment, that platform may need to handle
the refund or billing issue.

Unrecognized Charges
--------------------

If you notice a charge that you do not recognize, check your purchase
history and email receipts before contacting support.

Consider whether:

* Another person in your household made the purchase.
* You used another email address.
* The transaction is connected to a Game Club membership.
* The payment was processed through PayPal.
* The purchase was made through Apple or Google.
* The charge is from an older transaction.

If you still cannot identify the charge, contact Big Fish Games through
the official support system and provide the transaction date and
amount.

If you believe your payment account has been compromised, contact your
bank or payment provider as appropriate.

Protect Your Financial Information
-----------------------------------

When contacting billing support, protect your personal and financial
information.

Never include the following in a normal support request:

* Full credit-card number
* CVV or security code
* Account password
* Banking password
* One-time authentication code
* Security-question answers

Only provide information requested through the verified Big Fish Games
support process.

Keep Your Support Request
-------------------------

After submitting a request, save the confirmation email and ticket
number. If support asks for additional information, respond through
the existing support request whenever possible.

Avoid submitting multiple tickets for the same billing issue. Keeping
the conversation in one ticket helps maintain the complete history of
the problem.

Tips for Getting Billing Support
---------------------------------

Follow these steps when contacting Big Fish Games:

#. Visit the official Big Fish Games Help Center.
#. Search for your billing problem.
#. Check your Purchase History.
#. Verify that you are using the correct account.
#. Check your purchase confirmation email.
#. Gather the transaction details.
#. Include the purchase date and amount.
#. Provide the game name and order number.
#. Clearly explain what happened.
#. State the resolution you are requesting.
#. Do not provide your password or complete card number.
#. Save your support-ticket information.
#. Follow up through the original support request.

Final Thoughts
==============

Getting Big Fish Games billing support is easier when you provide
specific information about the transaction and clearly explain the
problem.

If you were charged for a game but cannot find the purchase in your
account, first check your Purchase History, other email addresses, and
purchase confirmation emails. If the transaction is still missing,
contact Big Fish Games support with the purchase date, amount, game
name, account email, and order number.

For payment problems, check your payment method and follow the current
support instructions. For refunds, review the applicable refund policy
before submitting your request. For mobile purchases, determine whether
Apple or Google processed the transaction.

Most importantly, use official Big Fish Games support channels and
protect your financial information. A clear, concise support request
can help the support team identify the transaction and provide the
appropriate solution.
